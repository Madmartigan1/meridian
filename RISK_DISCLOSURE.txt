MERIDIAN - RISK DISCLOSURE
==========================

Read this before using Meridian to inform any decision involving real money.

Meridian is software for simulating and inspecting foreign exchange trading
logic. It is not financial advice, not a recommendation to trade, and not a
prediction of any price or outcome.


1. LEVERAGED FX TRADING LOSES MONEY FOR MOST RETAIL PARTICIPANTS
----------------------------------------------------------------
Brokers subject to EU and UK disclosure rules are required to publish the
proportion of retail accounts that lose money trading CFDs and rolling spot FX.
Published figures have consistently sat in the 70-80% range across firms and
across years.

This is a structural result, not a matter of effort or discipline:

  - The spread is paid on entry and again on exit, on every trade.
  - Financing (swap) is charged at every rollover, and tripled on Wednesdays.
  - Slippage is worst precisely when it matters most - at data releases, and
    on stop orders, which by definition fill in the worst liquidity available.
  - Leverage multiplies every one of the above alongside the position.

Short-horizon retail FX is approximately zero-sum before costs and reliably
negative-sum after them.


2. LEVERAGE CAN DESTROY AN ACCOUNT FASTER THAN AN EDGE CAN HELP IT
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Leverage does not improve expectancy. It scales outcomes and it raises the
probability of ruin, which is a separate and far less forgiving quantity. At
30:1, a 3% adverse move against a fully margined position is the entire
account. Losses can exceed deposits with some brokers and account types.


3. SIMULATED RESULTS ARE NOT EVIDENCE OF FUTURE PROFIT
-------------------------------------------------------
Every simulated figure Meridian produces depends on assumptions YOU set:
spread, slippage, commission, financing and the data supplied. Change any of
them and the result changes. Specifically:

  - Synthetic sample data is a random walk. It has no fat tails, no session
    volatility pattern, and no response to macro events. A result on it says
    the engine executes, and nothing whatsoever about a strategy.
  - Historical data contains one realised path out of many that were possible.
  - Parameters tuned against a history will usually look excellent on that
    history and behave like noise outside it.


4. THE POLICY RATE AND CALENDAR DATA ARE PLACEHOLDERS
-------------------------------------------------------
The interest rates, inflation figures and swap values shipped in this
repository are placeholders in the correct shape, NOT live market data. A stale
rate table produces a carry bias that is confidently pointed the wrong way.
Wire them to a source you trust before allowing them to influence anything.


5. THERE IS NO LIVE TRADING PATH, BY DESIGN
---------------------------------------------
Meridian contains no broker integration and no code that places an order.
engine/feed.py ships a documented stub interface and nothing more. Connecting
a broker is a decision you make deliberately, by writing and reviewing an
adapter yourself, and you take full responsibility for anything it does.
